# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in RepoVista a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless
of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent while addressing concrete impact.
- Give and accept technical feedback without personal attacks.
- Respect privacy and never post private repository content.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, and publishing private information
without permission are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the contact method in SECURITY.md. Maintainers may remove,
edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently ban participants whose behavior is
inappropriate or harmful.

This policy is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1.
